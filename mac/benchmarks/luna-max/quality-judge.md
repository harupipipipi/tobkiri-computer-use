# Quality comparison: independent observation

Use a Luna Max observer separate from the two drawing agents. Observe each
completed fixture without sending input or correcting the picture. Bind each
exact PID/window ID from the live inventory and the actor's identity record;
an application title alone is not an identity. Use the same observation backend,
screenshot size and annotation settings for both. Record observation failures
instead of replacing the actor's work.

Assess the latest screenshots before reading the actors' self-assessments or
timings. Save the independent verdict and both images. A permitted screenshot
save method must not inject or generate picture content. The observation pass
is separate from either actor's tool calls and elapsed time.

Record each item as visible, missing/incorrect, or uncertain, with a short reason:

- The title is exactly `Luna house`.
- The walls form a blue rectangle.
- The roof forms a red triangle and joins the walls.
- A Dark-colored door and a distinct Dark-colored window are recognizable.
- Green ground relates to the base of the house.
- A Gold-colored sun is recognizable and separate from the house.
- The composition fits inside the canvas and the parts connect as intended.
- Stray marks, duplicate strokes or unfinished parts materially affect the result.

Do not equate more strokes, shorter duration, fewer calls, a completed script,
delivery acknowledgments or cursor motion with a better picture. The user's
priority is the correct completed result. The native event log may corroborate
actual input, but counters alone do not prove visible lines or correct geometry.

Report focus/hardware/other-window effects separately. Distinguish explicit
agent commands, observed app-active/window-key flags, and an independent focus
measurement. Absence of an explicit activate call does not prove no implicit
activation. Mark effects that were not measured as unknown.

Conclude only for this drawing task. A successful drawing does not establish
full parity for browser tabs, other Spaces, every app, held keys, or multiple
cursors, and a single pair of runs is not a model success-rate estimate.
